Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

📅 Effective Date: March 1, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Scriptgo. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

🔒 Your privacy is our priority. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect your name and email when you contact us, script inputs you enter (processed in real-time, not stored permanently), device information such as browser type and IP address, and usage data such as pages visited.

3. Your Scripts & Creative Content

When you generate a script, your input is sent to our AI model to generate a response. We do not permanently store your prompts or generated content. Generated scripts belong entirely to you.

✦ Your creative ideas are yours. Scriptgo does not claim any ownership over content you generate.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to provide and improve our service, respond to your messages and support requests, and analyze usage patterns. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to any third party.

5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential website functionality and anonymized analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us at supportscriptgo@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

Scriptgo is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
